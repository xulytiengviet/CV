# Selected projects

The portfolio combines academic research, geospatial engineering, Vietnamese language technology and open-source experimentation.

## GeoAI & GIS

**GeoAI Orchestrator / Behavior-Grounded Geospatial Reasoning**  
Research architecture for converting natural-language goals into validated GIS operations and, where appropriate, grounded robot actions.

**Open WebGIS & PMTiles infrastructure**  
Reusable browser-first GIS components, long-lived basemaps, vector tiles, administrative data and offline-capable geospatial applications.

**Environmental monitoring systems**  
GIS dashboards and spatial analysis for carbon, agriculture, aquaculture and environmental-resource applications.

## Language & compact AI

**CVNSS4.0**  
A Vietnamese writing/encoding research program explored as a canonical intermediate representation for typing, compression, speech and NLP experiments.

**BILATiny**  
Compact language-model experiments targeting constrained devices, wearables, smart-home systems and robots.

**BilaScript**  
An educational and experimental programming-language/tooling project, including browser exercises and JavaScript-oriented execution paths.

## Embodied geospatial systems

**Bila Robot / GIS-aware robotics**  
Simulation and architecture work connecting maps, sensors, behavior planning, state estimation, sensor fusion and SLAM.

**Làng Mê Kông simulation**  
Multi-agent and GIS-aware simulation concepts for environmental scenarios and local community contexts.

## Research infrastructure

**GIS2026 proceedings tooling**  
Repository-centered workflows for organizing, maintaining and publishing GIS conference/proceedings materials.

**LibPub / research publishing automation**  
Experiments around document-to-web scholarly publishing, structured metadata and reproducible release workflows.
