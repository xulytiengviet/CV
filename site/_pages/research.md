# Research program

My research treats **geography as the organizing layer** connecting environmental processes, data, sensing, AI and autonomous action.

## Environmental Geography + GIS

The foundation is environmental geography: understanding where processes happen, how they vary through space and time, and how geospatial evidence can support planning and management.

Current interests include environmental and natural-resource GIS, Mekong Delta spatial systems, carbon/agriculture/aquaculture applications, administrative and planning geodata, reproducible spatial-data pipelines, open WebGIS and long-lived geospatial basemaps.

## GeoAI and geospatial reasoning

GeoAI is developed here as more than prediction on raster or vector layers. The goal is a **GeoAI Orchestrator** capable of turning natural-language intent into auditable spatial workflows.

```text
User question
    ↓
LLM / planner
    ↓
Geospatial tool routing
    ├── PostGIS
    ├── GDAL
    ├── DEM / remote sensing
    ├── OSM / vector tiles / PMTiles
    └── domain APIs
    ↓
Spatial validation
    ↓
Map + evidence + explanation
```

## Sensing, IoT and identification

My doctoral background in IoT supports work on distributed environmental sensing, field devices, RFID and chipless RFID, and data pipelines that connect physical observations to GIS.

## Drone, robot and embodied GIS

The next step is to move geospatial reasoning from a screen into physical action: state estimation, sensor fusion, SLAM, GIS-aware navigation, action grounding, digital twins and world models.

## Vietnamese NLP/LLM

Work around **CVNSS4.0**, compact language processing, ASR/TTS and lightweight models explores how language can become a practical intermediate representation for devices, agents and geospatial systems.

## Unifying question

> How can an intelligent system reliably move from **language → geographic reasoning → coordinates → validated action** while preserving traceability, efficiency and human oversight?
